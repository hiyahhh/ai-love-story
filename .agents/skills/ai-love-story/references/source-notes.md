# Lessons from the Higgsfield tutorial

Source: [How to Create AI Love Stories (Full Tutorial), @adilinthewild](https://higgsfield.ai/@adilinthewild/blogs/youtube-guide-ai-lovestory-video). Reviewed November 10, 2026. These are paraphrased production notes, not a copy of the author's prompts or downloadable skill.

## What the article demonstrates

- Build reusable character sheets before video: portrait, front full body and back full body, with identity and clothing held constant.
- Establish location plates with stable geography and light direction. Use scale sketches or storyboards where spatial relationships are difficult.
- Give the scene-writing assistant the script, references and element list. Names in the resulting prompt must match the assets attached in Higgsfield.
- Reuse the technical block across scenes. Describe individual cuts, timing, camera, acting tasks, physical action and sound.
- Split complex sequences into separate generations; choose the best takes in the edit.
- The examples use Seedream 5.0 Pro for character sheets, Soul Cinema for locations, GPT Image 2 for a scale sketch and Seedance 2.5 for scenes. Those are the article's choices, not verified requirements or a guarantee of current availability.
- The author describes combining a real portrait with a generated costume sheet to improve recognition. Treat that as an optional manual reference workflow, and inspect lighting and proportions before use.

## How we adapt it for wedding films

Start with one meaningful encounter and a short present-day payoff. Save multiple eras, action scenes and crowds for later projects: they multiply continuity work. Begin with a 5-second test and a 30-second pilot; these lengths are our proposed defaults, not article requirements.

Use neutral partner identifiers rather than fixed gender roles. Preserve each person's actual features and chosen clothing. Keep factual memories separate from invented or symbolic scenes, and ask the couple to review the interpretation.

Track prompt version, model/settings, reference versions, cost and selected take. The article supplies the creative pipeline; our templates add production traceability and a repeatable handoff.

## Limits

Reference sheets reduce drift but do not guarantee likeness. Detailed prompts do not guarantee timing, physics or exact cuts. Review actual outputs and simplify or split a shot when it repeatedly fails. Model capability, price, available duration and element syntax must be checked in the chosen tool before generation. No generation service has been connected or tested in this repository.
